How to use Glow Pod
◎
The pod
Tap to enter breathing mode. The rings pulse gently outward. Tap again while breathing to cycle colors.
☀
Today's color
Each day a color is chosen for a reason — tied to the season, a date, or a story worth sitting with.
⬡
Gallery
Ten curated colors with a backstory and prompts. Tap any swatch to cast it onto the pod.
◈
Eyedropper
Drag across the spectrum to mix any color. Name it and save it. It becomes yours.
◇
Journal
Save any color with a personal note. Tap a saved dot to load that color back onto the pod.
▦
Calendar
Every journaled color appears as a colored dot on the calendar — a living record of your moods.
▷
Color story
Tap play and Aria reads you a warm, personal meditation written for your active color.
◐
Light / Dark
Toggle between light and dark. The color wash fills the entire window in both modes.
